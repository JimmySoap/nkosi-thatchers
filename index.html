What we do

Thatching services

From a new lapa to a full re-thatch, we cover the thatch work most Gauteng homes and estates need.

  • New thatch roofs

    Purpose-built thatch roofs for homes, rondavels and outbuildings, framed and thatched from the ground up.

  • Lapas & entertainment areas

    Open-sided lapas, gazebos and poolside braai areas — designed, framed and thatched to suit your garden.

  • Re-thatching

    Stripping tired, worn thatch and re-laying a fresh coat to bring an ageing roof back to life.

  • Repairs & maintenance

    Patching leaks, replacing ridging and top-ups to keep an existing thatch roof watertight and neat.

  • Ridging & capping

    Renewing the capping along the ridge lines — usually the first part of a thatch roof to wear.

  • Fire & waterproofing treatment

    Fire-retardant treatment using Thatch Marshall (SABS approved), backed by a 5-year certificate.
